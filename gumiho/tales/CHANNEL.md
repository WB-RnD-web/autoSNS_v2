## 채널 설명 (영어, 1,000자 이하)

```
Nine Tails Tales explains the most extreme places, depths and what-ifs on Earth and beyond, with real numbers and real sources.

How long would you last at the bottom of the Mariana Trench? What happens 8 minutes after the Sun disappears? Why can't anyone live in the hottest place on Earth?

Every Sunday: one 10–12 minute deep dive. Ranked countdowns of places you can't survive, zone-by-zone trips into the deep sea and the sky, and impossible scenarios played out minute by minute.

Your narrator is Gumi: very old, very dry, and impressed by almost nothing.

Every episode is researched from sources like NASA, NOAA, USGS and peer-reviewed studies, listed in each description. Visuals and narration are AI-generated illustrations, not real footage.

New long-form every Sunday at 10 AM ET. Shorts every day.
```
